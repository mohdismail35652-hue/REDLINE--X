HOW TO PLAY

START: ride as far as you can. Every 500 m is a new level with faster, denser traffic, and you get a hull repair.
Hold ACCEL to speed up, BRAKE to slow, steer between lanes. Tap HORN to scare slow riders out of your lane.
Hold BOOSTER for a speed burst; it refills while released.
MISSIONS: finish a goal to win coins. MAPS: pick your road.
Keyboard: A/D steer, W/S accel and brake, Space booster, H horn, Esc pause.
